Your friends' studying next to yours, on the Decks screen.

Due Crew is for small groups of friends, not global rankings. Swap a 6-character code with someone and you'll see each other's day: reviews, study time, retention, streak. That's the whole idea.

<b>Features</b>

<ul>
<li>Today and This week views, sortable by any stat</li>
<li>Shared deck progress — decks you have in common (AnKing included) match automatically, with seen/mature bars for everyone</li>
<li>Cheers — send 🎉 💪 🔥 and your friend gets a full-screen flurry with your name on it (click it to cheer back)</li>
<li>Friend profiles — click a name for their half-year heatmap and how many of the same days you've both studied</li>
<li>Crew Wrap — a weekly "together we did X" banner, plus streak milestone toasts</li>
<li>Consent-based friendships: stats flow only after both people add each other's codes, and removing someone cuts them off immediately</li>
<li>Privacy tab: share only the stats you want, or pause sharing ("on a break") — your streak keeps counting as long as you keep studying</li>
<li>Crew servers: run your whole group on your own free Firebase project — one person sets it up, everyone else joins with a name and a code</li>
<li>Follows Anki's night mode</li>
</ul>

<b>Light by design.</b> No background polling: the board refreshes when Anki syncs or when you click Refresh. All network runs in the background, so it never freezes Anki.

<b>Getting started:</b> Tools → Due Crew → sign in, then swap codes with a friend from the Friends screen.

Open source (MIT): https://github.com/sammyc2472/due-crew
