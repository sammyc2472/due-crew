# AnkiWeb listing draft

Title: **Due Crew — study with your friends**

---

Your friends' studying next to yours, on the Decks screen.

Due Crew is for small groups of friends, not global rankings. Swap a
6-character code with someone and you'll see each other's day: reviews,
study time, retention, streak. That's the whole idea.

**Features**

- Today and This week views, sortable by any stat
- Shared deck progress — decks you have in common (AnKing included) match
  automatically, and everyone's progress shows as seen/mature bars
- Cheers — send 🎉 💪 🔥 and your friend gets a full-screen flurry with
  your name on it
- Friendships are consent-based: stats flow only after both people add each
  other's codes, and removing someone cuts them off immediately
- Privacy tab: share only the stats you want, or pause sharing ("on a
  break") — your streak keeps counting as long as you keep studying
- Follows Anki's night mode

**Light by design.** No background polling: the board refreshes when Anki
syncs or when you click Refresh. All network runs in the background, so it
never freezes Anki.

**Getting started:** Tools → Due Crew → sign in, then swap codes with a
friend from the Friends screen.
