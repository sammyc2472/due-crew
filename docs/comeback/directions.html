Comeback characters

Four directions for the comeback plan. The big frame in each is Working, halfway through a 5-day comeback: 912 piled up against a usual day of 150, 183 extra a day, 366 won back and 96 of today's 183 done. The small ones are Before you go, Today's done and Caught up.

Scout

Climbs back one step a day; where it has walked turns green.

Colours are tokens only: --accent, --accent-ink (the faces, the board's ink-on-accent), --ink, --muted, --line, --card and --warm (the board's hours amber). Flat fills, no gradients, outlines or shadows; every square's corner is 18% of its side. Text stays in HTML above the drawing, so it keeps its size at 360 px while the drawing scales.
