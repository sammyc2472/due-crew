Your friends’ studying next to yours, on Anki’s Decks screen.

Studying for a big exam can get lonely. Due Crew is a free Anki add-on that puts your friends’ studying next to yours, so you can keep each other going.

  • Send a cheer, and it rains down their screen.
  • Open a study room and take the breaks together.
  • Leave a good-luck line for exam morning.
  • Only people you’ve added see anything, and only once they’ve added you back.
In Anki: Tools → Add-ons → Get Add-ons, then
